Executive Summary

Two vulnerabilities have been disclosed in the Ubiquiti UniFi Network Application, which could allow attackers to take over accounts and compromise underlying systems.

The primary issue (CVE-2026-22557) is a unauthenticated path traversal vulnerability with a CVSS score of 10.0 (Critical). A secondary issue (CVE-2026-22558) enables privilege escalation via NoSQL injection.

These vulnerabilities pose a significant risk, especially for instances exposed to internal networks or the internet.

CVE-2026-22557 — Path Traversal (Critical)

Type: Unauthenticated Path Traversal CVSS: 10.0 (Critical) Impact: Unauthorized access to sensitive system files Potential extraction of credentials or configuration data May lead to account takeover or full system compromise Attack Requirements: Network access to the UniFi application (no authentication required)

CVE-2026-22558 — NoSQL Injection (High)

Type: Authenticated NoSQL Injection Impact: Privilege escalation Abuse of application logic Potential lateral movement within the UniFi environment Attack Requirements: Valid low-privileged account

Exposure & Threat Landscape

Exposure counts and statements about exploitation below reflect the report published on 21 March 2026.

According to Censys, approximately 87,000+ UniFi instances are exposed online.

Version detection from external scanning is limited, making it difficult to determine patch status.

At the time of reporting: No confirmed public exploit (PoC) No confirmed active exploitation in the wild

However, due to the critical severity (CVSS 10.0), exploitation is considered highly likely in the near term.
